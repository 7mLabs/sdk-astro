installed package layout. New upstream versions, unlisted files and future

## Third-party material and combined distributions

Swiss Ephemeris code, its data, the font and derived font outlines, other
dependencies and their notices retain their respective licenses. Supplied
logos and brand assets are not included in this MIT grant. This permission
does not grant trademark rights. The separately MIT-licensed UI renderer
retains its own license.

The public native-package route continues to be AGPL-3.0-only, as recorded in
the package metadata and full `LICENSE`. The native libraries include Swiss
code and Chiron data. Neither `MIT` nor `AGPL-3.0-only OR MIT` describes the
license of that entire bundled native distribution. The additional permission
for 7mlabs material does not remove obligations that arise from combining it
with material belonging to another copyright holder.

For the Professional route, recipients must separately have Swiss permission
covering the actual use, modified source, data and combined distribution.
This MIT permission removes restrictions attributable solely to 7mlabs'
copyright in the identified material. It does not supply, expand or replace
a Swiss Ephemeris Professional License, waive an applicable Swiss agreement,
or establish that any particular prebuilt archive can automatically be used
under that route. The recipient's applicable Swiss terms govern; obtain
clarification from Swiss where the scope of a modified native build is unclear.
